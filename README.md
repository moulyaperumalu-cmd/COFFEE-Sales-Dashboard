# Coffee Sales Dashboard (Power BI)


## Project Name :
Coffee Sales Dashboard using Power BI


## Project Description :
The Coffee Sales Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze coffee shop sales data and provide meaningful business insights. The dashboard helps business owners understand sales performance, customer purchasing patterns, product demand, and monthly sales trends through easy-to-understand visualizations.
The dashboard presents important KPIs such as Total Sales, Total Transactions, Average Price, Sales by Month, Sales by Product, and Sales by Time of Day, allowing users to make informed business decisions.


## Project Objective :
The main objective of this project is to:
•	Analyze coffee shop sales performance. 
•	Monitor monthly sales trends. 
•	Identify the best-selling coffee products. 
•	Understand customer buying behavior based on different times of the day. 
•	Track important business KPIs. 
•	Support data-driven decision making using interactive visualizations.

## Dashboard KPIs :
The dashboard answers the following business questions:

#### 1. What is the Total Sales?
Measures the overall revenue generated by the coffee shop.

#### 2. How many Total Transactions were completed?
Shows the total number of customer purchases.

#### 3. What is the Average Price per Transaction?
Calculates the average amount spent by customers.

#### 4. How do Sales change every Month?
Displays monthly sales trends to identify growth and seasonal patterns.

#### 5. Which Coffee Products generate the highest sales?
Ranks products according to their total sales.

#### 6. During which Time of Day are sales highest?
Compares sales between:
•	Morning 
•	Afternoon 
•	Night 

#### 7. Which month records the highest sales?
Helps identify peak business periods.

#### 8. Which products contribute the least to total sales?
Identifies products that may require promotional strategies.


## Process :
The following steps were followed to build this dashboard:
#### Step 1:
Collected the Coffee Shop Sales dataset.
#### Step 2:
Imported the dataset into Power BI.
#### Step 3:
Performed data cleaning using Power Query by:
•	Removing null values 
•	Correcting data types 
•	Renaming columns 
•	Handling duplicate records 
#### Step 4:
Created calculated measures using DAX, including:
•	Total Sales 
•	Total Transactions 
•	Average Price 
#### Step 5:
Designed interactive visualizations including:
•	KPI Cards 
•	Line Chart 
•	Donut Chart 
•	Horizontal Bar Chart 
#### Step 6:
Applied a coffee-themed dashboard design using colors, icons, and images to improve visual appearance.
#### Step 7:
Reviewed the dashboard to ensure all calculations and visuals displayed accurate information.


## Project Insights :
The dashboard provides the following insights:
•	Total Sales reached 115.43K. 
•	A total of 3.636K transactions were completed. 
•	The Average Transaction Price is 31.75. 
•	Sales peaked during March, indicating strong customer demand. 
•	Sales decreased significantly during April before gradually recovering. 
•	Latte is the highest-selling coffee product. 
•	Americano with Milk and Cappuccino also contribute significantly to revenue. 
•	Espresso has the lowest sales among all products. 
•	Sales are distributed almost equally across Morning, Afternoon, and Night, indicating consistent customer traffic throughout the day. 

 ## Dashboard Overview :
The dashboard consists of the following sections:
#### 🔹 KPI Cards:
•	Total Sales 
•	Total Transactions 
•	Average Price 
These cards provide a quick overview of business performance.
#### 🔹 Sales Trend by Month:
A line chart compares:
•	Sales Amount 
•	Number of Transactions 
This visualization helps identify monthly growth patterns and seasonal fluctuations.
#### 🔹 Sales by Time of Day:
A donut chart displays sales distribution across:
•	Morning 
•	Afternoon 
•	Night 
This helps understand customer purchasing behavior throughout the day.
#### 🔹 Sales by Product:
A horizontal bar chart ranks coffee products according to total sales, making it easy to identify best-selling and least-selling products.
